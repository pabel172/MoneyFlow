#​💸 MoneyFlow
​Master your capital. Track your journey.
​MoneyFlow is a sleek, intuitive personal finance companion designed to help you take control of your financial life. Whether you're saving for a big goal or just trying to see where your coffee budget goes, MoneyFlow turns complex numbers into clear insights.
​🚀 Key Features
​Effortless Logging: Record income and expenses in seconds with a streamlined interface.
​Smart Categorization: Organize your spending into custom categories like Housing, Food, Entertainment, and Freelance Income.
​Visual Analytics: Understand your habits through beautiful, easy-to-read charts and spending breakdowns.
​Budgeting Goals: Set monthly limits for specific categories and get notified before you overspend.
​Transaction History: A clean, searchable ledger of every dollar that moves in or out of your pocket.
​Data Security: Your financial data is private and secure, ensuring your peace of mind.
​📊 Why MoneyFlow?
​"Financial freedom is available to those who learn about it and work for it."
​Most finance apps are cluttered and intimidating. MoneyFlow focuses on the "flow"—the movement of your money—making it easy to see the big picture without getting lost in the weeds.
